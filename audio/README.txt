The commissioner's anthem goes here.

Save your sound file in this folder as:

  commissioner-theme.mp3

It plays on the Los Puntos de Pina page and loops until the visitor
leaves the page or presses the button in the bottom right corner.

Using a different name or format (.m4a, .ogg, .wav)? Open
teams/puntos.html, find the line starting with <audio, and change the
src to match your file.

Note on browsers: Chrome, Safari and Firefox all refuse to play sound
that starts on its own until the visitor has clicked something on the
page. That is a browser rule the site cannot switch off. So the anthem
starts the instant they click anything, including the 'Meet the
commissioner' button, and the corner button starts or stops it.
