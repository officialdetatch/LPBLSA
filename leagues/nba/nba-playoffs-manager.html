NBA playoffs manager

This runs the bracket on the NBA Playoffs page. 1. Pick who is seeded No. 1, No. 2 and so on (or press Fill from standings to take them straight from the table). 2. As each game is played, click the team that won in the bracket below — it moves on to the next round and the other team is marked eliminated. Click the winner again to undo it. The teams that did not get a seed show as Fuera de playoffs on the page. When you are done, press Download nba-playoffs-data.js and drop the file into leagues/nba/assets/, replacing the old one. Nothing changes on the public site until you do. Visitors never see this page unless you send them the link — delete leagues/nba/nba-playoffs-manager.html and leagues/nba/assets/nba-playoffs-manager.js before uploading if you would rather it never leaves your computer.
