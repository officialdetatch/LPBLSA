Socials writer

Put posts from Threads, X.com, Instagram and TikTok in the Socials section at the bottom of the home page. Open the post on its own page, copy its address from the address bar (or use the platform's Share → Copy link), and paste it in the box below. The drop-down picks the platform from the link for you — just check it says the right one — and press Add post. The newest post goes first; use the ↑ ↓ arrows to reorder, and Remove takes one off. The preview at the bottom is the real thing, so what you see there is what visitors will see. Last step: press Download socials-data.js and drop the file into assets/, replacing the old one. Nothing changes on the public site until you do.

Posts have to be public. A TikTok link needs the long address (the one with /video/ and a number), not the short vm.tiktok.com one. Visitors never see this page unless you send them the link — delete socials-writer.html and assets/socials-writer.js before uploading if you would rather it never leaves your computer.
